# Abstract definition of constraints

a constraint is at the most abstract level, a set of possible solutions maybe in the classic lagrangian way (points in space). Here you are going to have some very basic things just a mapping from each task to periods of times. Now there you have constraints (descirption of what this task can and can not do). Each of those introduce a condition that cannot be violated (two tasks can not be in the schedule at the same time).

anything that reduces the subset of possible (feasable solutions)
